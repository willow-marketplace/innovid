## 0.1.0

Initial release.

- `business-health-check` skill: CFO-style briefing across Profit & Loss, Cash Flow, Balance
  Sheet, A/R Aging, and Sales reports (read-only).
- `industry-benchmark` skill: compare the business or a provided metric against regional and
  national industry peers (read-only).
- `setup` skill: guides users through connecting the QuickBooks connector and troubleshooting
  the connection.
